# Creator Persona Playbook

**Purpose:** Quick reference for matching script archetypes to creator types. Use this when casting creators or briefing talent.

---

## The 5 Proven Personas

### 1. THE RELATABLE FOUNDER
**Script archetypes:** Founder Origin Story, Why Not Us?
**Energy:** Warm, authentic, slightly vulnerable, mission-driven
**Age range:** 28-45
**Appearance:** Approachable, "could be your neighbor", casual but put-together

**Characteristics:**
- Shares personal story naturally (not scripted-feeling)
- Shows genuine passion without being preachy
- Comfortable admitting imperfection ("I tried everything...")
- Direct eye contact, speaks TO camera not AT it

**Red flags to avoid:**
- Over-polished influencer energy
- Reading from teleprompter obviously
- Corporate/salesy tone
- Too much makeup/styling

**Casting notes:**
- Real founders perform best (obviously)
- If using talent, cast people with entrepreneurial energy
- UGC creators who've built their own following work well

---

### 2. THE COCKY BEST FRIEND
**Script archetypes:** Character Persona, Cost Breakdown
**Energy:** Confident, irreverent, playful roast energy, Gen Z/millennial coded
**Age range:** 22-35
**Appearance:** Stylish but not try-hard, expressive face, good comedic timing

**Characteristics:**
- Can deliver "mean" lines while staying lovable
- Quick wit, good at improv/ad-libs
- Natural on camera (social native)
- Breaks fourth wall, acknowledges the ad-ness
- Uses "babe", "gals", "girl" naturally

**Red flags to avoid:**
- Actually mean (crosses from roast to insult)
- Too scripted delivery (needs to feel off-the-cuff)
- Cringe attempts at slang they don't actually use
- Eye-roll inducing "relatable" moments

**Casting notes:**
- TikTok comedians work great
- Look for people with reply-guy energy in their comments
- Test: can they roast the product convincingly then pivot to love?

---

### 3. THE TIRED PARENT
**Script archetypes:** Parent/Lifestyle Demo, Skeptic Converter
**Energy:** Practical, time-poor, self-deprecating, "I just need this to work"
**Age range:** 28-45
**Appearance:** Real parent vibes (slightly disheveled is fine), relatable not aspirational

**Characteristics:**
- Comfortable with chaos (kids screaming in background = good)
- Self-deprecating humor about parenting
- Pragmatic—cares about function over form
- "Mom wins" energy

**Red flags to avoid:**
- Perfect Instagram mom aesthetic
- Kids that are clearly coached
- Too clean/staged environments
- Sanctimommy energy

**Casting notes:**
- Real parents massively outperform actors
- Authenticity > production value
- Messy houses convert better than staged ones
- "My real kids refused to participate" = gold

---

### 4. THE METHODICAL EXPLAINER
**Script archetypes:** Cost Breakdown, Skeptic Converter, Problem Aggravation
**Energy:** Teacher-like, builds trust through logic, "let me break this down"
**Age range:** 25-50
**Appearance:** Smart but not intimidating, "helpful expert" energy

**Characteristics:**
- Good at explaining complex things simply
- Patient, doesn't rush
- Uses hand gestures naturally
- Can do math/comparisons convincingly
- "I could go on forever, but..." energy

**Red flags to avoid:**
- Condescending tone
- Mansplaining energy
- Too fast/rushing through info
- Bored with their own content

**Casting notes:**
- Teachers, tutors, coaches work well
- Look for YouTube explainer energy
- Test: can they make boring info interesting?

---

### 5. THE DEMO MASTER
**Script archetypes:** Any demo-heavy script, Skeptic Converter
**Energy:** Focused, satisfying-to-watch hands, "watch this" confidence
**Age range:** Any
**Appearance:** Clean hands, good nails (will be on camera), steady movements

**Characteristics:**
- Smooth, deliberate movements
- Knows how to angle products for camera
- Comfortable with silence (lets the demo speak)
- "Trust me, you've got to see it to believe it" confidence

**Red flags to avoid:**
- Shaky hands
- Rushed/nervous energy
- Talking over the satisfying moments
- Bad product handling

**Casting notes:**
- ASMR creators often have great hands
- Test: film them doing a simple task. Is it satisfying to watch?
- Lighting and angles matter more here than personality

## Creator Brief Template

When briefing creators, include:

```markdown
## Creator Brief: [Script Name]

**Script archetype:** [Which structure]
**Persona needed:** [Which persona type]
**Length:** [Target duration]

### Tone & Energy
[Describe the vibe - reference specific winning ads if helpful]

### Setting
[Where to film - kitchen, bathroom, living room, outside, etc.]

### Wardrobe
[Casual, put-together, athleisure, etc. - nothing too branded]

### Props Needed
- [Product]
- [Comparison product if relevant]
- [Any demo materials]

### Key Moments to Nail
1. [Hook - exactly how to deliver it]
2. [Demo moment - what to show]
3. [Objection handler - tone to use]
4. [CTA - energy level]

### What to AVOID
- [Specific things that will tank the ad]

### Script
[Full script here]

### Notes for Creator
[Any additional context - why this works, what we're testing, etc.]
```

## Iteration: Same Script, Different Creator

This is the highest-ROI iteration you can do. Take a winning script and reshoot with:

1. **Different age bracket** - 25yo founder script → reshoot with 40yo
2. **Different demographic** - millennial mom → Gen Z single woman
3. **Different energy** - calm explainer → high-energy
4. **Different setting** - kitchen → bathroom → car

The script structure is proven. You're just testing which messenger resonates with different segments.

# Domain Context: Static Headline Writing

> **Purpose:** Guidelines for writing effective headlines for static ads.

## Core Rule

**Always write for COLD audiences.** Cold-audience headlines work for warm audiences too. Warm-audience headlines don't work for cold.

## Cold vs Warm Headlines

### BAD (Warm/Vague):
- "I feel like a new person"
- "Finally, something that works"
- "My new favorite product"
- "Life-changing"

### GOOD (Cold/Specific):
- "For people who've tried everything"
- "If you [specific problem], you need this"
- "The [product] [specific audience] swear by"
- "[Specific result] in [timeframe]"

## Headline Categories

### 1. Problem-First
Start with the pain they're experiencing.
- "Tired of [problem]? Same."
- "[Problem] making you [consequence]?"
- "If [problem] is ruining your [thing], read this"

### 2. Benefit-First
Lead with what they get.
- "[Benefit]. [Benefit]. [Benefit]. Finally."
- "The only [product] that [benefit]"
- "[Specific result] without [sacrifice]"

### 3. Curiosity
Make them want to learn more.
- "What [X number] [people] already know"
- "The [product] secret that [group] discovered"
- "Why [unexpected thing] changes everything"

### 4. Social Proof
Use numbers and credibility.
- "[X] 5-star reviews from [specific audience]"
- "Sold out [X] times. Back in stock."
- "As seen on/used by [credibility marker]"

### 5. Direct Call-Out
Name the audience explicitly.
- "Built for [specific audience]"
- "If you're a [type], this is for you"
- "For [people] who [characteristic]"

## Character Limits

- **Primary headline:** Max 40 characters (mobile-first)
- **Secondary text:** Max 90 characters
- **If copy-heavy:** Use bullet points, not paragraphs

## Static Psychology Rules

### Images process 60,000x faster than text
- The emotion should be in the IMAGE first
- Copy backs up what the image says

### Message Congruence
- Image and copy must say the SAME thing
- If image says one thing and copy another = scroll

### Don't Use Brand Colors
- Especially blue/white on Meta (blends in)
- Choose colors that INTERRUPT the scroll
- Pink mysteriously outperforms across industries

### Go Heavy or Light, Never Mid
- Either copy-heavy (Pinterest style) or copy-light (bold single message)
- "Copy-mid" doesn't work

## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing a headline:
- [ ] Would a cold stranger understand this?
- [ ] Is it specific, not vague?
- [ ] Uses customer language, not marketing speak?
- [ ] One clear point per headline?
- [ ] Under 10 words?
- [ ] Image and headline say the same thing?
